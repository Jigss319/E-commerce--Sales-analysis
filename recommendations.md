# Business Recommendations 
## 1. Increase Stock of Best-Selling Products
Drawing Book was the best-selling product with 77 units sold.
The Business should maintain sufficient inventory of Drawing Books to avoid stockouts and lost sales.The company can also promote this product through bundles and cross-selling to increase overall revenue.
## 2. Strenghten the Home Category
The home category generated the highest revenue of 8,64,051 ruppes,
The business should maintain a strong product selection in this category and consider increasing marketing efforts for high-performing Home product to maximize revenue.
## 3. Review Low-Performing Products
Sticky Notes generated only 2,970 ruppes in revenue,indicating weak sales performance.
The business should review its pricing,promotion,and product positioning. if performance remains low, the company can consider reducing inventory or replacing the product with a better-performing alternative.
