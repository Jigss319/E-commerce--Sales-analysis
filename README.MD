# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview 
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using SQL to identify sales performance,customer behavior,product performance,and category-level revenue.
The analysis helps the business understand its top customers, best-selling products,high-performing categories,and low-performing products to support better business decisions.

## Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Identify the top customers by revenue.
Identify the best-selling product categories.
Identify low-performing products.
Provide actionable business recommendations based on the analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
SQLite- Database management and SQL analysis
SQL - Data quarying and business analysis
Python - Data generation and database interaction
GitHub - project documentation and portfolio presentation

## Key Insights
1. Overall Sales
The E-commerce business generated a total revenue of 29,47,730 ruppes from 300 orders.
This indicates the overall sales volume and revenue generated during the analyzed period.
2. Top 5 Customers
The analysis identified the top 5 customers based on their total spending.
These high-value customers represent important customers for retention and loyalty strategies.
3. Best- Selling Product
Drawing Book was the best-selling product with 77 units sold, including strong customer demand.
4. Best- Performing Category 
The Home category generated the highest revenue of 8,64,051 ruppes, making it the strongestrevenue-generating category.
5. Low-Performing Product 
Sticky Notes generated only 2,970 ruppes in revenue, making it one of the weakest-performing products and an area for improvement.

## Business Recommandations
Based on the analysis, the following recommandations can help improve business performance:
   . Increase stock of best-selling products such as Drawing Book to avoid stockouts and lost sales.
     Promote these products through bundles and cross-selling.
   . Stremgthen the Home category by maintaining a strong product selection and increasing marketing efforts for high-performing products.
   . Review low-performing products such as Sticky Notes by evauating pricing,promotion,and product positioning.Consider redusing inventory or replacing consistently weal products with better-performing alternatives.
